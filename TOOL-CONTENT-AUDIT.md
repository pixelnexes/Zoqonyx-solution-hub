## 1. Content Architecture Standards

Every active tool page in Zoqonyx adheres to a structured, editorial and technical blueprint designed for maximum user utility and top organic search positioning:

1. **Meta Title Tag:** Specific, benefit-driven, incorporating primary target search terms and brand suffix (`| Zoqonyx`).
2. **Meta Description:** Clear summary under 160 characters describing exact capabilities, file limits, client-side privacy guarantee, and zero sign-up friction.
3. **Hero Section (H1 & Subtitle):** Distinct H1 matching user intent; contextual badges showcasing privacy (`Zero Cloud Uploads`) and speed.
4. **Interactive Utility Canvas:** Clean, responsive tool interface rendered immediately without distracting promotional banner clutter.
5. **How-It-Works Step-by-Step Guide:** Clear 3-to-4 step operational instructions.
6. **Technical Specifications & Limitations:** Transparent disclosures regarding browser memory, supported MIME types, and device hardware constraints.
7. **Comprehensive FAQs:** 4 to 6 detailed questions and answers marked up with JSON-LD `FAQPage` schema.
8. **Related Utilities Grid:** Contextual navigation cards guiding users to related workflows in the same category.
